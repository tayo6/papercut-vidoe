Subject

The background is keyed out from the photo's edges, and transparent images are used as they are. To fix the rest, pick a click tool and tap the preview: it removes or keeps the connected area of similar colour. Brushes paint by hand.

Paper fold

The sheet is folded along a grid of creases and opens like a map: each flap swings over the top, lit and shadowed, and shows the blank back of the paper as it turns. One column and one row is a plain sheet. Open time and Hold are in seconds; Crease marks stay on the flat sheet.

Magazine letters

Each pass builds fresh scraps with a new font, colour and paper for every letter. Assemble and Hold are in tenths of a second. Flicker swaps letters in place that many times a second.

Newspaper highlight

One keyword per line. Each keyword runs through that many different clippings, with the word locked at the centre of the frame. Draw time is the share of each beat spent drawing the highlight.

Match cut

The cut-out stays locked in the centre of the frame while the paper behind it changes on every beat.

Transition

Length is the share of each beat spent on the transition.

Text morph

Hold and Morph are in tenths of a second. Each word is rebuilt from paper scraps that slide into the next word.

Word orbit

Words on the far side of the sphere fade back. Copies repeats the phrase to fill the sphere. Rings applies to the Rings layout.

Paper

Motion

Boil redraws the torn edges a few times a second, cycling through a small set of drawings the way hand-cut stop motion does. Animate on steps the whole animation like frames shot on twos.

Video editor

A timeline editor: import photos, video and music, trim and split clips, pick in, out and looping animations, move and rotate on the canvas, turn any clip into cut paper, and export a video.

Cut-out

Photo to torn paper with click-to-select, five edge styles, eight paper textures, distortion, shadow, grain and boil.

Unfold

Hinged paper panels swing open in the direction you pick, with creases, flex and camera tilt. Works for photos and typed notes.
